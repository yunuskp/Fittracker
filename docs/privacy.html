FitTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2, 2026

Overview

FitTracker is designed as a local-first workout tracking app. Your workout data is stored on your device and is not sold, rented, or used for third-party advertising or tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

FitTracker may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Exercise names, categories, and icons
  • Workout weights, sets, repetitions, notes, and dates
  • Workout templates, goals, and app preferences

Notifications

FitTracker can send local notifications with your permission, including workout reminders, personal record celebrations, and rest timer alerts.

You can turn notifications off at any time in the app settings or iOS Settings.

Subscriptions and Purchases

FitTracker Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

FitTracker does not receive or store your full payment information. Subscription status is checked using Apple StoreKit.

Data Security

Workout data is stored locally using Core Data and protected by iOS device security features.

FitTracker does not require an account and does not operate a user data server for workout history.

Children’s Privacy

FitTracker does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Important changes will be reflected in the app or published with the updated policy.
